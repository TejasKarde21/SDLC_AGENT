Project Summary

Project NameHospital Management System
ObjectiveTo provide a comprehensive platform for patients to register, book appointments, and manage medical records, while enabling doctors to handle appointments, create EMRs, and process insurance claims efficiently.
Actors
  • Patient
  • Doctor
  • Insurance Company
Modules

  • Doctor Management
  • Appointment Management
  • EMR Management
  • Insurance Claim Management

Functional Requirements

ID Module Actor Requirement Acceptance Criteria
REQ-PAT-001 Patient Management Patient Register a new patient account with personal details and optional insurance information. Patient receives a confirmation email and can log in immediately after registration.
REQ-PAT-002 Patient Management Patient Login to the system using registered credentials. System authenticates credentials and redirects to patient dashboard.
REQ-PAT-003 Doctor Management Patient Search for doctors by specialty, name, or location. Search results display doctor profiles with availability status.
REQ-PAT-004 Appointment Management Patient Book an appointment with a selected doctor for a specific date and time. Appointment request is queued and a notification is sent to the doctor.
REQ-DOC-001 Appointment Management Doctor Receive appointment requests and view details. Doctor can see patient information, requested time, and reason for visit.
REQ-DOC-002 Appointment Management Doctor Accept or reject appointment requests. Appointment status updates to accepted or rejected and patient is notified.
REQ-EMR-001 EMR Management Doctor Create an EMR record after consultation. EMR is linked to the patient and appointment and is stored securely.
REQ-EMR-002 EMR Management Doctor Add diagnosis, medicines, and services to the EMR. All entries are validated and saved; EMR can be viewed by the patient.
REQ-CLAIM-001 Insurance Claim Management System Generate an insurance claim if the patient has insurance coverage. Claim data is compiled from EMR and patient insurance details.
REQ-CLAIM-002 Insurance Claim Management System Send the claim to the insurance company via the agreed interface. Claim is transmitted successfully and a receipt is logged.
REQ-CLAIM-003 Insurance Claim Management Insurance Company Approve or reject the claim. Insurance company response updates claim status and notifies the patient and doctor.

Non-functional Requirements

  • The system must be available 99.9% uptime.
  • All data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • The application must comply with HIPAA regulations.
  • The system should support at least 10,000 concurrent users.
  • Response time for search and booking operations must be under 2 seconds.
  • The UI must be accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA).

Business Rules

  • Only registered patients can book appointments.
  • Only doctors with verified credentials can accept appointments.
  • An appointment can only be booked for a future date and time.
  • Claims are generated only if the patient has active insurance coverage.
  • Insurance claims must include diagnosis codes, medication codes, and service codes.

Validations

  • Email must be in a valid format.
  • Password must be at least 8 characters, include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character.
  • Appointment date/time must not be in the past.
  • Diagnosis, medicine, and service codes must exist in the master code list.
  • Insurance policy number must be validated against the insurer's database.

Assumptions

  • Patients provide accurate insurance information during registration.
  • Doctors maintain an up-to-date availability schedule.
  • Insurance companies expose a RESTful API for claim submission.
  • The system will integrate with an external email/SMS service for notifications.

Dependencies

  • External email service provider (e.g., SendGrid).
  • External SMS gateway.
  • Insurance company claim API.
  • Database system (e.g., PostgreSQL).
  • Authentication provider (e.g., OAuth 2.0).

Clarification Questions

  • What specific fields are required for patient registration beyond basic contact information?
  • Should patients be able to reschedule or cancel appointments, and if so, what rules apply?
  • What format and data fields are required by the insurance company for claim submission?
  • Is there a requirement for patient or doctor portal access via mobile devices?
  • How should the system handle appointment conflicts if multiple patients request the same slot?
